Ledger warnings now use kind-specific summaries, translations and advice instead of treating every non-migration warning as vacuity. Deadlock, coverage and incomplete probes retain their distinct meanings; unknown kinds remain visible generic warnings. Existing vacuity and migration rows, raw evidence, assurance and exit behavior are preserved. Affected approvals report `drifted` for `rendering_changed` (exit 0) and require review and re-approval.
