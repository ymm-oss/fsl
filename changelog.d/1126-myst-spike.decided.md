Decided (#1126): mystmd is not adopted for `docs/`. The spike record `docs/DESIGN-myst-spike.md` measures the destruction-case table it would have to pass — every one of five reference breaks exits 0 under `myst build --site --strict` until `error_rules` and `--check-links` are configured, and no single switch closes the table — alongside the three costs that decide it: `--check-links` rejects the correct directory links in the docs index, mystmd's heading slugs disagree with GitHub's on four anchors that resolve today, and the build depends on four external hosts. `tools/check-doc-links.py` is the counter-proposal, resolving in-repository link targets and GitHub-slugged anchors with no network; it is committed with its own destruction rows and is deliberately not wired into a gate.
